# Steam store copy

## Short description

SNACKRUN is a low-poly gig-work driving game. Take delivery offers, read the
city, keep gas in the tank and the car in one piece, and chase the next run while
the radio plays. Build your shift one drop at a time.

## Long description

**Clock in. Find the address. Make the drop.**

SNACKRUN is an open-city delivery driving game about the rhythm of gig work. Your
car is your livelihood, the tank is always getting lower, and the next offer is
always waiting.

Pick up food, drive across a procedurally built city, park, get out, and hand it
over before the timer runs out. Spend the money on fuel and repairs, then decide
whether to take one more run or call it a night.

### FEATURES

- **Gig-work delivery loop** — accept offers, collect the order, beat the clock,
  and get paid.
- **A car that needs care** — fuel, collision damage, wear, and limp-home
  breakdowns. Repairs cost money. Bad driving costs more.
- **Get out and move** — park, walk, run, enter buildings, and complete the
  handoff on foot.
- **Two levels** — start with a compact city shift, then move into the bigger
  map with a second drivable vehicle and a storage facility.
- **Living shifts** — day/night cycle, sleepiness, sleep, meditation, gas
  stations, traffic, and a persistent bank balance.
- **In-game radio** — a built-in station, the INFINITE EP station, plus optional
  Spotify and SoundCloud playback on your own accounts.
- **Presence-only multiplayer** — share a room code, see other drivers in the
  city, and talk over proximity voice chat.
- **Controller support** — drive with triggers and sticks; keyboard and mouse are
  always available.

### A SHIFT, NOT A RACE

There is no finish line. There is a tank, a timer, a bank balance, and a city
full of addresses. SNACKRUN is about the small decisions between them.

## System requirements

**Minimum**

- OS: Windows 10 64-bit
- Processor: Dual-core 2.0 GHz
- Memory: 4 GB RAM
- Graphics: WebGL 2 capable GPU
- Storage: 2 GB available space
- Network: Broadband connection for multiplayer and optional music services

**Recommended**

- OS: Windows 11 64-bit
- Processor: Quad-core 2.5 GHz
- Memory: 8 GB RAM
- Graphics: Dedicated GPU with WebGL 2 support
- Storage: 2 GB available space
- Network: Broadband connection

## Controller note

The driving and gameplay controls are playable on a controller. Some settings,
account setup, and text-entry flows still need a keyboard. The current Steam
controller-support label should be **Partial Controller Support**.

## Multiplayer note

Multiplayer is co-presence, not shared progression. Players keep their own
deliveries, money, fuel, health, radio, and save data. Voice chat is optional and
uses a proximity relay.

## Legal/credits note

SNACKRUN includes optional integrations with Spotify and SoundCloud. Those
services require their own accounts and are subject to their terms. All bundled
music must be licensed for distribution with the game.
